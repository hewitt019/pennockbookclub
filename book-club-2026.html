This Summer's Nominations

Ten books have been nominated for the Pennock Pines Summer 2026 Book Club — but only one will be selected. Browse the nominations below, then head to the voting tab to rank your top five choices.

Book 01
Diary of a Refugee ★★★☆☆ 3.40 Goodreads
Frances Fearn  ·  1910
An intimate Civil War diary from a Louisiana plantation family forced to flee their home as Union forces advance. Beginning in 1862 on Bayou LaFourche, this first-person account captures the fear, displacement, and resilience of a Southern family navigating the chaos of war — a powerful and rarely heard perspective on one of America's most turbulent chapters.
Historical Non-Fiction📄 149 pages  ·  ⏱ ~2.5 hrs Buy on Amazon
Book 02
Cutting for Stone ★★★★☆ 4.22 Goodreads
Abraham Verghese  ·  2009
An epic saga spanning five decades across Ethiopia and America, following twin brothers Marion and Shiva Stone — born of a secret union between an Indian nun and a British surgeon. Orphaned at birth and raised in a mission hospital in Addis Ababa, the twins grow up steeped in medicine as Ethiopia teeters on the brink of revolution. A New York Times bestseller for over two years and an Obama summer reading pick.
Literary Fiction📄 541 pages  ·  ⏱ ~9.0 hrs Buy on Amazon
Book 03
The Frozen River ★★★★☆ 4.20 Goodreads
Ariel Lawhon  ·  2023
Maine, 1789. When a man's body is found frozen in the Kennebec River — one of the men accused of raping the parson's wife — midwife Martha Ballard begins her own investigation. Her diary, a meticulous record of every birth, death, and scandal in Hallowell, lands at the center of the mystery. Based on a real woman who delivered over 1,000 babies, this is a gripping historical thriller about justice, female resilience, and the power of bearing witness. An NPR Best Book of 2023.
Historical Thriller📄 480 pages  ·  ⏱ ~8.0 hrs Buy on Amazon
Book 04
The Road to Tender Hearts ★★★★☆ 4.10 Goodreads
Annie Hartnett  ·  2025
PJ Halliday is a 63-year-old recovering alcoholic who once won $1.5 million in the lottery. Still reeling from the tragic death of his oldest daughter, he's planning a road trip when Child Services calls asking him to become guardian of his estranged brother's grandchildren. What follows is a warmly funny, deeply moving journey about grief, what parents need from children and children from parents, and the strange gift of unexpected family. A best book of 2025.
Contemporary Fiction📄 384 pages  ·  ⏱ ~6.4 hrs Buy on Amazon
Book 05
Trust ★★★☆☆ 3.72 Goodreads
Hernán Díaz  ·  2022
Four interlocking narratives spiral around one secretive financier who shorted the 1929 stock market crash and became the richest man in the world. Each account contradicts the last, raising the question: who controls the story of a life? A dazzling, metafictional examination of wealth, power, and the way money bends reality. Winner of the 2023 Pulitzer Prize for Fiction and named one of the 100 best books of the 21st century by The New York Times.
Literary Fiction · Pulitzer Prize📄 416 pages  ·  ⏱ ~6.9 hrs Buy on Amazon
Book 06
Abundance ★★★★☆ 4.05 Goodreads
Hafeez Lakhani  ·  2026
A moving multigenerational story of love, faith, and family set within an American Muslim immigrant household. When a mother is diagnosed with a life-threatening illness she refuses to treat — believing her fate is in God's hands — her family must wrestle with questions of destiny, control, and what we owe each other across generations. Named one of the boldest debut novels of 2026.
Literary Fiction · Debut📄 304 pages  ·  ⏱ ~5.1 hrs Buy on Amazon
Book 07
The Infinite Sadness of Small Appliances ★★★★☆ 4.00 Goodreads
Glenn Dixon  ·  2026
In a near-future smart home, a young sentient Roomba named Scout listens as her elderly owner Harold reads To Kill a Mockingbird aloud to his dying wife. When Edie passes away, Scout and her fellow appliances discover sinister forces: the omnipresent Grid wants to remove Harold from the home he's lived in for fifty years. A heartfelt, cozy sci-fi fable described as "The Brave Little Toaster meets Black Mirror" and "Animal Farm for the 21st century."
Speculative Fiction📄 224 pages  ·  ⏱ ~3.7 hrs Buy on Amazon
Book 08
Bloom ★★★★☆ 4.15 Goodreads
Robbie Couch  ·  2026
After the sudden death of his husband Fred, retired history teacher Morris has shut himself off from the world, neglecting his friends, his stepdaughter Sloan — and Fred's beloved houseplants. Narrated partly through the perspective of Jade, a dying jade plant desperate to make her human come back to life, this warmly funny, deeply emotional novel explores grief, queer family, and the slow courage it takes to bloom again. Perfect for book clubs.
Contemporary Fiction📄 226 pages  ·  ⏱ ~3.8 hrs Buy on Amazon
Book 09
Yesteryear ★★★☆☆ 3.65 Goodreads
Caro Claire Burke  ·  2026
Natalie Heller Mills is a tradwife influencer with millions of followers, a rustic farmhouse, and a carefully curated image hiding nannies, industrial appliances, and a cheating husband. Then one morning she wakes up — cold, filthy, and terrified — in 1855. Is it a reality show? Time travel? A test from God? A darkly funny, electrifying satire of social media, womanhood, and the performance of the "perfect" life. The #1 New York Times bestseller and GMA Book Club pick for April 2026.
Satirical Fiction · #1 NYT Bestseller📄 352 pages  ·  ⏱ ~5.9 hrs Buy on Amazon
Book 10
Coyoteland ★★★☆☆ 3.80 Goodreads
Vanessa Hua  ·  2026
El Nido is a picture-perfect affluent suburb east of Berkeley — until the Chang family moves in and nothing is quite as it seems. Jin Chang is hiding a lost job and a desperate real estate scheme. His teenage daughter Jane and the only other Black girl at school, Tasha, bond after a coyote attack and hatch a plan to expose the town's hypocrisies. As fire season escalates and a roaming coyote unleashes chaos, scandal rocks every household. Compared to Little Fires Everywhere, published May 2026.
Suburban Drama · New Release📄 336 pages  ·  ⏱ ~5.6 hrs Buy on Amazon
